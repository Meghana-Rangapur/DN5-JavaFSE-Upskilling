CSS Ex 9

Community Bulletin

The city council is pleased to announce the upcoming Summer Gala, a flagship event celebrating our community's diversity and talent. Residents from all wards are invited to participate in the festivities planned across three days.

Local vendors will showcase handmade crafts, regional cuisine, and live performances. The organizing committee has confirmed that parking will be available at the Central Grounds and shuttle services will run every 20 minutes from major bus stops.

Volunteers are urgently needed for event setup, crowd management, and information desks. Interested residents can sign up through this portal's registration page or visit the ward office between 10am and 4pm on weekdays.

Safety and accessibility remain top priorities. Ramps, sign-language interpreters, and dedicated quiet zones will be available. Medical personnel will be on standby throughout the three-day event. All participants are encouraged to carry valid ID.

In addition, the council reminds residents of the ongoing tree plantation initiative every Sunday morning at the Riverside Park. Saplings are provided free of charge and refreshments will be served to all volunteers who show up before 7am.

The next town hall meeting is scheduled for the last Friday of the month. Agenda items include the proposed community library extension, repair of footpaths in Ward 4, and updates to the digital portal. All suggestions are welcome in writing before the meeting date.
